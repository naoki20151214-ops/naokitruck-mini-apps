## Numbers, money, dates
- Dates like 2026.10.25 stay as they are. 2026年10月 → October 2026.
- 円 → yen. 1兆円 → ¥1 trillion; 500万円 → ¥5 million; 年収400万〜600万円 → annual salary of ¥4–6 million.
- 億 = 100 million (10億 = 1 billion). Convert carefully.
- Units (nm, °C, %, etc.) stay the same.
