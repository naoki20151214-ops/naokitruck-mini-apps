Launched September 7: recording pressure changes during polishing

On September 7, 2026, Fujifilm announced that it would launch "Prescale Motion," a dynamic surface pressure sensor system, that same day. The product visualizes how the pressure on a contact surface changes from the start to the end of pressing. In the semiconductor field, the company lists as an example use the need to check how the contact state and load between the head and the wafer change during wafer polishing. Fujifilm launch announcement

This news is not a future development concept but the maker's announcement that sales have started. However, a product launch and adoption in mass production at semiconductor fabs are separate events. The announcement does not name semiconductor customers, the number of units delivered, or improvement figures for polishing quality or yield. What can be confirmed at this point is the launch of the measurement system and its functions and intended uses. Details of the launch announcement

What do "surface pressure" and "dynamic measurement" mean?

Surface pressure is the pressure on surfaces that are in contact. For beginners, it helps to think of dividing the surface into small sections and looking at how strongly each one is pressed. What is evaluated is not just one representative value for the whole surface but the distribution, meaning where pressure is concentrated. Prescale Motion uses a surface pressure sensor made of a dedicated film and a controller, combined with a mobile device. Product overview

Dynamic measurement tracks how a state changes over time. For example, it is a way of looking at a sequence such as "at the start of pressing, the pressure shifted to one side, and then the distribution changed." This is not a polishing case confirmed with this product but an example to explain what time information means. The maker shows a way of evaluating that combines static data at maximum pressure from its earlier product with dynamic data from Motion. Comparison of static and dynamic data

The link to wafer polishing lies in this question of "when did it change." Fujifilm describes a need to record pressure changes between the head and wafer from the start to the end of polishing. So the significance here seems to be that it adds time information to the pressure distribution on the contact surface, giving more clues for examining the state in the middle of the process. This is a significance read from the measurement functions, not a result that proves fewer polishing defects. Description of use for semiconductor wafer polishing

What changed from the earlier color-developing film?

The technical background is the earlier pressure measurement film, "Prescale." When pressure is applied, tiny capsules inside break, and the dye inside reacts with a developer and turns red. The depth of that color is used to evaluate the pressure. The calibration chart that matches color to pressure is chosen based on the temperature and humidity at the time of measurement. In other words, measurement conditions also need to be checked when reading the color. Structure and use of the earlier Prescale

Motion, on the other hand, gets data over time from a dedicated surface pressure sensor. If you think of it as a product that simply films ordinary color-developing film on video, you miss the difference. Also, the principle of the earlier film, where capsules break and develop color, cannot be described as is as the detection principle of the dedicated Motion sensor. The maker describes a setup that combines the earlier static evaluation with the new dynamic evaluation. Prescale Motion setup and evaluation method

How to read the specs of 1,024 points and about 30Hz

According to the official specifications as of September 2026, there are 1,024 measurement points (32×32), the measurement frequency is about 30Hz, and AD conversion is 8-bit. Hz is a unit for times per second, so about 30Hz means about 30 measurements per second. AD conversion is the process that turns the sensor signal into digital numbers. 8-bit means values are expressed in 256 steps, but that alone does not determine how accurate the pressure measurement is. These are the maker's nominal specifications. Main specifications

ItemNominal specification
Measurement size80mm square, 200mm square
Element size2.5mm and 6.25mm, respectively
Film thickness0.2mm or less
Recommended pressure range0.5–2.5MPa, with a note that it can vary with sensitivity adjustment
Data functionsMeasure, display, save, play back, and TSV export

The values and functions in the table are based on the official specifications. Measurement size and element size are conditions that relate to how large an area is examined and at what level of detail. The time resolution of about 30Hz also has limits. So you cannot conclude from the count of 1,024 points alone that it can capture every tiny contact unevenness or brief change.

You also need to be careful about the units of the numbers. In the product overview, the "total pressure" and "average pressure" in dynamic data use bit as the unit and are defined as the sum and average of the 8-bit values in an area. They cannot be read directly as values in MPa, the unit of pressure. The ability to compare distributions and changes and the accuracy with which absolute pressure values can be measured need to be checked separately. Definitions of measurement items

What to look for next to judge the effect in mass production

Even though semiconductor wafer polishing is included as an example use, that does not show it can be applied as is to every polishing tool. From this announcement and the product overview and specifications alone, we cannot confirm resistance to polishing slurry, how it is installed during rotation, specific compatible tools, or the absolute pressure accuracy of dynamic measurement. Whether the pressure and measurement range of the process you use fit the nominal specifications also needs checking. Announcement of uses, Specifications related to application conditions

Going forward, it will be worth watching for adoption cases at real semiconductor customers and what could be measured under which conditions. To judge the effect on process improvement, beyond being able to record pressure changes, you need comparisons showing how polishing quality and yield changed after adjustments using that information. The evidence at this stage is the maker's launch and feature description, not independent performance verification. It is best to see this news as the arrival of a product that adds more information for evaluating the polishing process. Launch announcement, Product overview
