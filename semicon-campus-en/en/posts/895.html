Announced September 10, with products and evaluation kits available now

On September 10, 2026, Renesas Electronics announced the RZ/G3L and RZ/G3SE, 64-bit MPUs for industrial control screens and IoT devices. The company said both products and their evaluation kits are available starting the same day. This is not just an announcement of a future launch. However, we could not confirm regional stock, pricing, lead times, or volume shipment quantities.Renesas announcement

The key points this time are the company's claim of power consumption around 1 mW in deep standby, and a design that makes it easier to share boards and software assets across products with different display features. Beyond the power figure itself, what matters is how a device goes into standby and how it comes back when needed.Announcement text, Company technical blog

What kind of chip is it? An MPU that runs screens and communications

An MPU is a microprocessor that handles the processing needed to run an OS and applications. According to the maker's published specs, both products run up to four Arm Cortex-A55 cores at up to 1.2 GHz and also include a Cortex-M33. A "core" is a unit that does processing, but you cannot judge how fast a finished device responds from core count or clock speed alone.Product announcement, Published specs

HMI, one of the listed uses, is the interface people use to check a machine's status and operate it. Think of a screen that shows a tool's status and lets you enter settings. Renesas's technical blog targets devices that need communications and data processing in addition to display and control. It describes rolling out models with different levels of display capability on a common development environment.Uses and development environment

"Around 1 mW" is a standby value. How is the Linux state kept?

1 mW is 0.001 W. However, this 1 mW figure is the maker's published value for deep standby, a deep sleep state. It is not the power used while the screen, communications, and data processing are running normally. From the information we could confirm, we also cannot tell which power circuits are included, or whether external memory and communication circuits are counted. Note that it does not mean "the whole finished device sits in standby at around 1 mW."Low-power feature announcement

The technical background is four independent "power domains." A power domain is a group of functions whose power can be controlled separately. Like turning off the lights room by room in a building, the idea is to separate the parts that are needed from the parts that can rest. The company's technical blog says this power control is combined with DDR memory self-refresh.Technical blog on power control

"Self-refresh" is a mode in which memory keeps running the internal operations needed to retain its data. Renesas says the Linux image is held in DDR and the system resumes in about 1 second. This does not mean Linux keeps processing normally during standby. It is best understood as a design that pauses processing while keeping the state needed to resume. The "about 1 second" figure is also a company claim, not an independent measurement of response time including the user's applications and peripherals.State retention and resume

RZ/G3L and RZ/G3SE differ in video features

The clearest difference when choosing between them is graphics and video processing. In the published comparison table, the RZ/G3L has a Mali-G31 for 3D graphics and a codec for H.264 video compression and decompression. The RZ/G3SE has neither. What you show on screen and how you handle video become the deciding factors.Product specs and comparison table

Still, it is not accurate to see the RZ/G3SE as "only for devices without a screen." Renesas includes IoT devices that need a simple display among its uses. Whether a chip has a 3D GPU or video codec is a separate question from whether the device uses a screen.Description of uses

For communications, the RZ/G3L's published specs list one lane of PCIe Gen2, two channels of TSN-capable Gigabit Ethernet, and CAN FD. These specs relate to connecting with external devices. However, supporting a connection standard does not by itself decide overall system communication performance, and the information we could confirm does not show whether the same communication features stay available during standby.Published interface specs

Learning control and evaluation from low-power MPUs

To turn standby power news into work or study, compare things on equal terms: standby versus active states, what was measured, and the resume conditions. Experience inspecting control equipment or keeping evaluation records is a good starting point for exploring equipment and quality jobs. If you study embedded software at home, tell the evaluation board apart from the host PC, and check the supported OS and required memory for the development environment before comparing PCs.

Checklist before comparing: supported OS and required memory for the development environment, evaluation board connection, and licenses. Separate what you can try with your current setup from what needs upgrading, then choose your options.

The value of a common board lies in designing for more models

Renesas explains that the two products are pin-compatible so they can share a common board, and a related blog also mentions software compatibility. "Pin compatibility" means the layout and roles of the pins connecting the chip to the board are compatible. For example, if a model with an advanced display and a model with a simple display can share a board design, it may reduce the work of redesigning for each model. This is a design benefit inferred from the published specs.Product information, Common design blog

On the other hand, the word "compatible" does not mean every package can be swapped without conditions, or that software works with no changes at all. We also could not confirm how much development cost or time is saved. Sharing a design is one option for reusing design assets, and the real effect must be judged against the features and product configuration you use.On compatibility and development assets

What to watch next: real-device standby power and resume conditions

In future evaluations, the first step is to check the measurement conditions and scope of the 1 mW figure. If we learn the power of the whole board including external DDR and communication circuits, and the resume time with a real OS and application setup, it becomes easier to judge whether the low-power design fits a given device. You cannot calculate battery life or energy savings from the published standby power and the roughly 1-second resume time alone.Standby power announcement, Resume feature

What we can confirm is the company's announcement that the products and evaluation kits are available, along with their specs and design approach. It has not shown adoption in mass production or independent verification of the power savings. The next focus is under what conditions the published values can be reproduced on evaluation kits and adopting devices, and whether a common board works for the intended feature set.Availability announcement, Design approach
