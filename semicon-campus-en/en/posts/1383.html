On September 29, 2026, six Samsung companies (Samsung Electronics, Samsung C&T, Samsung SDS, Samsung SDI, Samsung Life Insurance, and Samsung Fire & Marine Insurance) announced they would invest a total of $1 billion in AI infrastructure company Helix Digital Infrastructure. Samsung Electronics will invest $500 million, and the other five companies will invest $500 million combined. What was announced is an investment plan. It does not mean that data centers or power plants have been completed, or that power supply has started.Samsung official announcement

What happened

Helix is an AI infrastructure company whose launch was announced on June 11, 2026. Its founding investors are KKR, the Kuwait Investment Authority, NVIDIA, and Vistra, and it is led by former AWS CEO Adam Selipsky. Helix plans to develop and operate hyperscale data centers and also handle power generation, transmission and distribution, fiber optics, and network connectivity as one package. At its launch, it also said it had more than $10 billion in long-term commitments.Helix launch announcement

Samsung's official release lists related capabilities within the group: semiconductors, data center cooling through FläktGroup, EPC by Samsung C&T, data center design and operation by Samsung SDS, and UPS and backup batteries by Samsung SDI. In other words, this investment can be read as a sign that Samsung wants to expand beyond supplying semiconductor components into the facilities and energy that support AI computing. However, no specific projects, customers, construction sites, power capacity, use of Samsung products, or revenue impact have been announced.Samsung official announcement

Why do AI data centers need so much power?

An AI data center does not run just by installing GPUs and memory. Large amounts of power must be brought in from power plants or the grid, and substations, grid connections, permits, and backup power must be set up. Even if the building is finished, servers cannot run until the power connection is complete. Cooling equipment to handle the heat from servers, networks linking sites, and a team for ongoing operation are also essential.

A hyperscale data center is a large facility for cloud providers and large AI services where many servers can be added in stages. What matters is not just the size of the building. Power, cooling, and networking must be able to expand as computing equipment grows. Helix has laid out a plan to develop and operate such facilities together with power and connectivity.Helix launch announcement

A UPS is an uninterruptible power supply. When a power outage or voltage fluctuation happens, it supplies power for a short time from batteries or other sources to keep servers from suddenly shutting down. Even with power from plants or the grid, a computer stopping because of a brief drop in power quality can cause big losses. That is why UPS units and batteries are equipment that supports stable operation of AI data centers.

Cooling is also important. AI servers packed at high density give off a lot of heat, so besides air cooling that circulates air, liquid cooling that carries heat with fluid and coolant distribution units may be used. Samsung likely listed its cooling, construction, operations, and energy storage capabilities because it sees business opportunities not only in semiconductors themselves but also in the surrounding equipment that runs them. This is the company's strategic explanation and does not show specific adoption results.Samsung official announcement

The industry background on power and AI

The International Energy Agency (IEA) has analyzed how the growth of AI and data centers could affect not only power demand but also power sources, energy security, emissions, and electricity prices. As AI computing grows, the challenge is not just chip performance and price, but whether power generation, transmission, cooling, storage, land, permits, and networks can all be built out at the same time.IEA: "Energy and AI"

What sets Helix apart is not inventing new power technology or semiconductors. It is trying to use long-term capital to coordinate data center development, power procurement, transmission and distribution, fiber optics, and operations in one business model. Samsung and Helix both explain that AI infrastructure requires coordinating many types of equipment at once, and Helix says it aims to secure capacity through direct investment in and partnerships with power developers.Samsung official announcement Helix launch announcement

Points to check next

To judge whether this announcement leads to real AI infrastructure, we need to follow the process from investment decision to equipment, contracts, and operation. The following information is especially important.

  • Whether Helix announces specific data center projects, regions, and customers
  • Whether contracts with power producers and utilities, grid connections, and permits move forward
  • Whether data center power capacity, cooling methods, and UPS and energy storage equipment are disclosed
  • Whether products and services from Samsung companies are used in real projects
  • Which of power, cooling, permits, or cost of capital becomes the constraint in each region

As of October 1, 2026, we could not find any follow-up corrections to the September 29 announcement or additional announcements of specific Helix projects on Samsung's official page or Helix's official news list. However, this does not rule out undisclosed contracts or future updates.
