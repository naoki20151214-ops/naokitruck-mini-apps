The earnings of companies that handle assembly and testing also offer clues about semiconductor demand. On September 10, 2026, ChipMOS announced unaudited consolidated revenue of NT$2,785.7 million for August 2026. That was up 33.3% from the same month a year earlier but down 1.3% from the previous month. The company cited strong memory demand as the background, but 33.3% is the growth rate of total company revenue, not the growth rate of its AI-related business alone.ChipMOS monthly announcement

Technical background: the back-end process and OSATs that handle assembly and testing

The keyword for understanding ChipMOS's role is the "back-end process." Here it means the steps such as semiconductor assembly and testing that take place before a product goes to the customer. A business that takes on this work for outside customers is called an OSAT (outsourced semiconductor assembly and test). ChipMOS describes a turnkey service that bundles wafer sort, assembly, final test, and drop shipment. It helps to think of it as a company that supports chipmaking through a division of labor.ChipMOS turnkey service

Another term is "wafer sort." A wafer is the disk-shaped material that semiconductor chips are made on, and wafer sort is the step that tests the chips on it to tell good from bad. "Final test," on the other hand, is the stage of testing products after assembly. The company's services include both, covering checks from the wafer stage to after assembly. However, this does not mean every product goes through the same steps in the same order.Official process description

This division of labor is what links memory demand to the results of back-end companies. ChipMOS's products include memory ICs. However, it also handles display driver ICs and logic and mixed-signal ICs. So total monthly revenue should be read as the combined result of several product areas.Product areas served

Testing relies on equipment and programs

Semiconductor testing needs "test programs" as well as equipment. A test program is a set of procedures and conditions for running the right tests for each product on the tool. ChipMOS says it develops and optimizes programs and also helps move them to different test platforms. For example, when moving to a different tester environment, the program is adapted so tests can run in that environment.ChipMOS test services

There are also several ways to provide the equipment. The company lists buying its own, holding equipment consigned by customers, and joint purchasing. From this, contract testing can be understood as a business that involves technical and equipment adjustments to fit each customer's products and equipment conditions. Note that this service page shows no publication or update date, and it does not indicate which procurement method will be used for the current expansion.Test support and equipment procurement

Why it matters: how memory demand affects the back-end process

ChipMOS said it is benefiting from strong memory demand amid an ongoing AI-related supply-demand imbalance. This news offers a way to connect memory demand in the AI era to the results of companies that do assembly and testing. However, this explanation of demand is the company's own assessment. It is not a figure showing how much AI demand lifted revenue, nor an independent third-party verification.Company view on memory demand

The announcement does not give AI-related revenue or the revenue contribution by memory type. The 33.3% rise in total revenue also cannot be treated as the memory segment's growth rate, shipment volume growth, or capacity growth. Expansion investment matches customer demand; scale and timing not disclosed

The company says it keeps discipline in expansion investment, matching new capacity to committed customer demand to meet customers' short- and long-term needs. We can confirm an investment stance of adding capacity in line with demand, but the investment amount, location, capacity increase, and specific start date were not given.Company explanation of capacity expansion

Here it is important to tell apart the investment policy, carrying out the investment, and the equipment starting operation. This announcement does not confirm that a capacity increase is complete or that new equipment has started running. At the same time, the lack of a specific schedule does not justify concluding that "the investment has not started" or that "it is only a policy." The reading that fits the disclosure is that this monthly announcement does not show how far any specific expansion has progressed.Expansion explanation and scope of disclosure
