On September 17, 2026, Infineon Technologies announced the EiceDRIVER 2EDL6014AC-G2D, a dual-channel gate driver for AI data center power supply design. The IC fits two independent drive channels into a 2.2 mm square body. The company says samples are available as of the announcement, but it did not give an exact availability date or mass production start date.Official announcement

The key point this time is how a small drive component supports power circuit design. On the other hand, the "120V" figure being promoted comes with temperature and supply conditions. You need to read the power-saving effect for AI servers separately from the specifications of the part itself.Aim of the announcement, Datasheet

What was announced

This product is an IC that drives external silicon MOSFETs. A MOSFET is a semiconductor switch that turns a power path on and off, and this product handles that switching. The two independent channels can be used both high-side, both low-side, or in a half-bridge setup that combines high-side and low-side. The body also has a pad for heat dissipation.Product page

September 17 is the date of the official news release. The datasheet has a revision date of July 1, 2026, so the news date should not be treated as the first publication date of product information or the first shipment date. Also, being able to get samples is different from confirmed volume supply or customer adoption.Official announcement, Technical document showing the revision date

What part of the power supply does a gate driver handle?

A gate driver is a component that moves charge into and out of the gate, the MOSFET's control terminal, to switch it. Think of it like a door: the control circuit decides when to open and close, the driver moves the door, and the MOSFET opens and closes the path for power. Separating these three roles makes it clear that a driver IC alone does not make a complete power supply.Gate driver technical overview

The 4A source / 6A sink specification shows the ability to push current into the gate and to pull it out. It is drive current for switching on and off, not the load current supplied to an AI processor. Just lining up these numbers next to those of power components that handle large currents does not let you compare power supply capability.Drive current specifications

Floating output is another important term. In a high-side switch, the reference voltage for driving moves up and down as the circuit switches. A floating output drives relative to that moving voltage instead of a fixed ground. The product's two independent outputs can be read as giving more design freedom when placing MOSFETs that operate at different reference voltages. This is about circuit configuration and does not indicate how much efficiency improves.Technical categories, Description of independent outputs

What miniaturization means for AI server power

Infineon promotes the design of two channels in a 2.2 mm square as a feature that supports higher density and thermal design in AI data center power supplies. Packing drive components into a small space is something to consider when placing power circuits on limited board area. However, the announcement has no comparative measurements showing rack density or power efficiency gains, and better performance in finished servers has not been shown.Official announcement

The technical background is circuits that convert voltage by combining several switches. The datasheet shows an example hybrid switched-capacitor circuit in which three of these ICs drive four high-side and two low-side MOSFETs. A switched capacitor circuit converts voltage by using switches to change how capacitors are connected. This example helps explain why independent drive channels are needed, but the diagram alone cannot tell you the efficiency or board area of a finished power supply.Datasheet with application circuit

The product page also describes a function that adjusts the OUTA drive current to limit inrush current at startup. Inrush current is a brief surge of current into capacitors and other parts when power is turned on. Being able to adjust startup behavior is a design feature, but how much it is limited in a real circuit must be evaluated separately.Description of product functions

From gate drivers to power supply evaluation conditions

When you read the 120V figure, separate the absolute maximum rating, the temperature conditions, and the operating conditions of the actual circuit. Do not use numbers from an article directly as safety settings for your own equipment; check them against formal design documents and your company's procedures. 120V is an absolute maximum rating with temperature conditions

If you take "120V" as a voltage you can use normally, you will misread the specifications. Table 3 of datasheet Rev. 1.0 lists the absolute maximum rating for VDDA and VDDB as 120V at 25 to 150°C, and 105V at −40 to 150°C. A footnote also adds the condition that VDDI is supplied within the recommended supply range. If you use it at low temperatures, 120V does not simply apply across the board.Datasheet Table 3, page 8

An absolute maximum rating is a conditional limit beyond which damage may occur. It is not a specification that guarantees normal operation at that value. Under the recommended operating conditions used for normal design, the upper limit of GNDA and GNDB relative to GNDI on the input side is 100V, and the gate supply relative to each floating reference is 4 to 16V. Because the reference terminals differ, it is important not to treat 120V, 100V, and 4 to 16V as voltages at the same point.Absolute maximum ratings and recommended operating conditions

The pin count also differs between documents. The official news says WQFN-12, while the current datasheet says WQFN-11 and the product page says QFN-11. Use the current datasheet for technical specifications, and also check the pin layout when designing the board. The available documents do not explain the cause of the discrepancy or whether a correction is planned.Official news, Datasheet, Product page

Next, watch for real-circuit evaluations and supply information

As a concrete evaluation tool, the official evaluation board combines this driver with external 80V MOSFETs to form a half-bridge buck circuit with a 36 to 60V supply range. It runs open loop, meaning the control that watches the output and adjusts automatically is not closed, and it provides waveform measurement points. This is useful for checking drive behavior, but the board's supply range cannot be taken as the rating of the IC itself or of a whole server.Evaluation board specifications

What to watch next is the mass production start date, pricing, customer adoption, and measured conversion efficiency and temperature in real circuits. These materials do not confirm any of these or third-party performance verification. At this stage, it makes sense to evaluate its design features as a small driver IC available as samples, and to wait for future measurements and adoption results before judging its effect on whole power supplies.Announcement details and availability
