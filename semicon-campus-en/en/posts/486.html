Learning level Beginner

For people who are starting to see the roles of the CPU, memory, and storage.

DRAM and NAND are both memory semiconductors, but they are not parts that do the same job. DRAM quickly moves data in and out while you work, and NAND keeps photos and apps even after the power is turned off. This article sorts out the differences in role, cells, uses, and manufacturing step by step, through a conversation between Naoki and Professor Owl and diagrams.

If you want to start with how they relate to the CPU, reading The difference between CPU, memory, and storage first will make this easier to understand.

What you will learn in this article
  1. The biggest difference between DRAM and NAND
  2. Why data disappears or stays when the power is turned off
  3. Their roles in PCs, smartphones, SSDs, servers, and AI
  4. Where cell structures and manufacturing processes get difficult
Naoki

If both DRAM and NAND store data, why not just use the faster one?

Professor Owl

You can't decide by speed alone. The role changes depending on whether the data is used quickly while working or kept after the power is turned off.

Naoki

DRAM is for working, and NAND is for saving. That's why you need both.

Professor Owl

Exactly. First, let's compare the big picture of the two with a diagram.

Why does DRAM lose data when the power is turned off?

Naoki

I get that it's for working. But why does it lose data when the power is turned off?

Professor Owl

DRAM represents 0s and 1s with the charge in tiny capacitors. The charge leaks little by little, so it must be read and written back again.

Naoki

So to keep its memory, it checks and rewrites over and over.

Professor Owl

That's called refresh. When the power is gone, refresh stops too, and the data can no longer be kept.

A typical DRAM cell is the "1T1C," which combines one transistor and one capacitor. It reads the charge state of the capacitor and refreshes it regularly. Because of this property, DRAM is classified as volatile memory.

Key points of the figure

  • The cell's charge decreases over time

Why does NAND keep data even when the power is turned off?

Naoki

NAND also uses charge, so why doesn't its data disappear?

Professor Owl

It stores charge in an insulated region and tells 0s and 1s apart by that state. Its structure keeps the charge from escaping quickly even when the power is turned off.

Naoki

That's why it can save photos and apps. But it doesn't last forever, right?

Professor Owl

Right. The SSD controller handles wear leveling, error correction, and more to support cell lifespan and data reliability.

Typical NAND methods include floating gate and charge trap. Both use the fact that the transistor's properties change depending on the charge state. The explanation so far of "telling 0s and 1s apart" is a simplification to help you grasp the mechanism. Some real products read multiple threshold voltage states and record multiple bits in one cell. Because it can keep its memory without frequent refresh, NAND is non-volatile memory.

NANDA semiconductor chip that stores data.
SSDA storage device that combines NAND, a controller, firmware, and more.
ControllerSpreads out writes, corrects errors, and manages reading and writing.

How are they used in PCs, smartphones, and SSDs?

Naoki

How do apps and photos move between DRAM and NAND?

Professor Owl

While saved, they sit on the NAND side. When you open an app, the needed data moves to DRAM, and the CPU or GPU processes it.

Naoki

So you spread things out on the desk only while working, and put the results you want to keep back in storage.

Professor Owl

Exactly. But the actual transfers and management are handled by the OS and various controllers working together.

When you open an app, the part of the data stored on the SSD or similar that is needed for the work is loaded into DRAM. The CPU or GPU processes it, and when you save the results you want to keep, they are written back to the NAND side.

PCs and smartphonesRunning apps go in DRAM; photos, videos, and the apps themselves go on the NAND side.
ServersBecause they handle many tasks at once, they combine large-capacity DRAM with fast storage.
SSDsUse NAND as the storage medium, with a controller hiding complex operations from the user.

In AI, which matters more: DRAM or NAND?

Naoki

I hear HBM is important for AI. Does that mean NAND is no longer needed?

Professor Owl

HBM is high-bandwidth memory based on DRAM. It helps supply data during computation, but NAND is still needed for long-term storage of training data and models.

Naoki

So even in AI, DRAM-type memory is for working and NAND is for saving.

Professor Owl

That's right. Systems are built by combining three things: how fast you compute, how fast you deliver data, and how much you store.

HBM is not a type of NAND. It is memory that stacks multiple DRAM dies and is mounted near a GPU or similar chip to get wide bandwidth. Its structure and advanced packaging are covered in a separate article.

Even among memory semiconductors, the hard parts of manufacturing differ

Naoki

Are DRAM and NAND made the same way in the fab?

Professor Owl

Many processes are shared, but the hard parts differ. For DRAM, it's the structure that holds charge in tiny cells. For 3D NAND, the big challenges are the many layers and deep vertical holes.

Naoki

Because their roles differ, the structures they aim for in deposition and etching change too.

Professor Owl

Exactly. Differences in companies, equipment, materials, inspection, and jobs also come into view from here.

Hard parts of DRAMHigh-aspect-ratio capacitors, thin films, materials, charge retention, and variation control.
Hard parts of 3D NANDMultilayer deposition, deep-hole etching, film uniformity, and alignment.
Shared hard partsKeeping defect reduction, inspection, electrical characteristics, and yield all at a high level.

Apply your understanding of DRAM and NAND to choosing a PC

Once you understand the difference between DRAM and NAND, figure out whether the problem with your PC is a lack of working memory or a lack of storage capacity. Check your usage and the standards your model supports, then compare adding parts with replacing the PC itself. Even with the same capacity, roles and connection methods differ. People with manufacturing or inspection experience can also broaden their understanding to the processes that make memory and the jobs that support the equipment.

Checklist before comparing: memory usage and free space, DDR generation and form factor, and SSD connection method. Separate what you can try in your current setup from what needs replacing before choosing candidates.

Frequently asked questions

Which is faster, DRAM or NAND?

In typical systems, DRAM has lower latency than NAND storage and can do fine-grained reading and writing at high speed. However, actual performance varies with generation, standard, connection, controller, and access method.

Is NAND memory or storage?

NAND is a memory semiconductor. An SSD, which combines NAND with a controller and other parts, is a storage product.

Can't DRAM be used for saving data?

Ordinary DRAM loses data when power is cut, so it is not suited to long-term storage. There are special backup configurations, but typical PCs use DRAM for working and SSDs for saving.

Summary

  • DRAM is volatile memory that holds data in use
  • NAND is non-volatile memory that keeps data even when the power is off
  • DRAM needs refresh
  • NAND holds a charge state, and the SSD controller handles wear leveling, error correction, and more
  • The difference in roles leads to differences in cell structure, manufacturing processes, companies, and jobs
