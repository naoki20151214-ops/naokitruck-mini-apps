Why is CMP equipment important?

CMP stands for Chemical Mechanical Planarization (also called chemical mechanical polishing). It is a process that flattens the wafer surface by combining chemical reactions from chemicals with the mechanical action of a polishing pad.

In semiconductor manufacturing, deposition, etching, and wiring formation are repeated, so steps build up on the surface. If the steps stay large, lithography has trouble focusing and wiring formation becomes difficult. That is why CMP is like "leveling the ground" so the next process can work.
