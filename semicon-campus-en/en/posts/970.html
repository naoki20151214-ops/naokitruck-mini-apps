What happened? 700nm wiring and vias formed on a 12-inch wafer

On September 10, 2026, Taiyo Holdings announced that, in joint research with the Belgian research institute imec, it had formed a three-layer RDL structure on a 12-inch wafer using its "FPIM series" semiconductor packaging material. The company says both the wiring and the vias that connect layers reached 700nm in a dimension measure called CD. According to the company, a co-authored paper was also presented the same day at IEEE ESTC 2026. September 10 is the announcement date and the conference presentation date; the dates the samples were made or the experiments finished have not been disclosed. Taiyo Holdings official announcement

This news is about progress in evaluating materials and processing technology for making the fine wiring that connects chips. "The company's first" in the announcement means a first for Taiyo Holdings; it does not claim a world first or the smallest in the industry. It also does not report better performance of a finished AI chip or the start of mass production. English announcement

What are RDL and vias? A way to reroute chip connections

An RDL (redistribution layer) is a wiring layer that reroutes a chip's electrical connections to where they are needed. In a building, it is like arranging where the electrical wires go to match the room layout. A via, on the other hand, is a connection that links wiring on different layers vertically. Even if horizontal wiring can be made thinner, large vertical connections still limit how small the whole structure can be. Reporting 700nm for both wiring and vias can be read as a result that evaluated the two together. Earlier structure explanation, Current evaluation results

700nm equals 0.7μm. CD (Critical Dimension) here is a measure of a key dimension of the formed pattern. It is a different number from the "process node" that describes transistor manufacturing technology. Wire width, the space between wires, and pitch (the distance from the center of one wire to the center of the next) are also all different. So this announcement cannot be restated as "700nm-pitch wiring." Explanation of published dimensions

Care is also needed in reading "three layers." In an earlier 2025 announcement, the company described a test structure made of two RDL layers and a via layer connecting them. Given this history, the phrase "three-layer RDL" should not be read directly as a structure with three layers of metal wiring. 2025 test structure

Why does it matter in the AI era? Technology to connect multiple chips

The background is the development of advanced packaging that connects multiple small chips, called "chiplets," at high density. imec explains that such connection technology affects system performance, power efficiency, and the amount of data that can be exchanged. Besides the chips that do the computing, how chips are linked to each other is also an important technical challenge. imec explanation of advanced packaging

Against this background, developing insulating materials that can form fine redistribution wiring is meaningful as a basic technology supporting high-density connections. However, the 700nm dimension alone cannot tell us how many times faster AI processing will be or how much power use will drop. A separate imec RDL document also does not show that FPIM is used, and the performance projections in that document cannot be treated as results for this material. Materials need more than the ability to draw fine patterns

FPIM is a light-sensitive insulating material for the dual damascene process. The company announcement describes it as a "negative-tone i-line photosensitive dielectric material." Damascene is a method of making wiring by filling trenches in an insulating film with metal. Picture filling grooves cut into a floor with metal and then leveling the surface, and the processing idea becomes easier to grasp. FPIM material description, Damascene technical explanation

The step that flattens the surface is CMP (chemical mechanical polishing). It combines chemical action and mechanical polishing to make the surface flat. So the material must not only form fine shapes but also suit the processing steps that follow. Taiyo Holdings says it confirmed high resolution and compatibility with the CMP step this time. The key point is that, besides drawing thin wiring, the company also evaluated how well it works with the steps that turn those shapes into a wiring structure. From RDL material progress to packaging and quality jobs

After reading a wiring formation announcement, sort out not only line width but also materials, formation conditions, interlayer connections, and inspection and reliability. It is important not to treat values from research as a guarantee for the whole mass production process. Experience in measurement and quality records, assembly, and equipment checks can be an entry point for researching packaging and process jobs. While relearning electrical, metrology, and quality topics, check the numbers and conditions in public English documents in the original.

What progressed since the 2025 evaluation?

The joint research began in October 2022. In the earlier announcement on November 13, 2025, the company reported a CD of 1.6μm for the two RDL layers and 2.0μm for the via layer on a 12-inch wafer. Comparing the published dimensions, this time's result of CD 0.7μm for both wiring and vias shows that both have become finer. However, these evaluations were done at different times, and we cannot confirm that the process conditions were the same. November 2025 announcement, September 2026 announcement

When thinking about how close this is to practical use, the purpose of shipments also needs to be distinguished. According to the company, it has been shipping small samples of FPIM for R&D since 2025. This means supply of evaluation material has started, but it is not information showing adoption in customer products or the start of mass supply. Explanation of sample shipments

Remaining challenges and what to check next

Taiyo Holdings says leakage current and resistance evaluations for M1 at CD 700nm were good. Leakage current is current that leaks to places where it should not flow. However, the public release gives no specific measured values, measurement conditions, or sample counts. The M1 evaluation cannot be extended into proof that the long-term reliability of all layers and all via connections is guaranteed, and dimensional uniformity across the whole wafer and yield cannot be confirmed either. The evaluation results are based on the company's announcement, not on independent replication. Published electrical properties

The next development goal is RDL formation at CD 500nm or below, and the company plans to continue long-term verification of electrical properties and reliability. 500nm or below has not been achieved this time, and no deadline has been given. Going forward, beyond further miniaturization results, specific information on stability over long use, manufacturing variation, customer adoption, and mass supply will be the basis for judgment. For now, it is best seen as progress in evaluating materials that support advanced packaging. Future development plan
