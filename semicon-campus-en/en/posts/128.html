The short answer: SCREEN is an equipment maker strong in cleaning and surface treatment

SCREEN Semiconductor Solutions' official product page introduces product lines such as wafer cleaning systems, wet stations, spin processors, spin scrubbers, and coater/developers. For beginners, it is easiest to first see it as "a company strong in equipment that cleanly treats the wafer surface."

In semiconductor manufacturing, each step such as deposition, lithography, etching, and CMP brings risks like particles, residues, chemicals, and metal contamination. Cleaning equipment is used to reduce this contamination and leave the wafer in a state where defects are less likely in the next process.

Why cleaning equipment matters

Semiconductor circuits are extremely small, so even tiny particles can cause defects. After etching, reaction byproducts and residues may remain. After CMP, slurry and polishing debris are left behind. Around lithography, managing resist and developer is also important.

In other words, cleaning is not a stand-alone process. It is closely tied to the steps before and after it. To understand SCREEN, it helps a lot to read an article about cleaning first.

How is it different from Tokyo Electron or Kurita Water Industries?

Tokyo Electron also handles several front-end process tools, including cleaning. SCREEN, on the other hand, is a company whose name tends to come up in the context of cleaning equipment. Kurita Water Industries and Organo are easier to understand in the context of ultrapure water and water treatment.

Even when they all relate to "cleaning," equipment, water treatment, chemicals, materials, and quality control play different roles. In company research, it is important to see which company supports the cleaning process from which position.
