On October 21, 2026, the semiconductor industry association SEMI announced a forecast that global silicon wafer shipments in 2026 will grow 11.8% year over year to 14.42 billion square inches (14,420 MSI). Shipments are expected to hit a new record every year from 2027 through 2029, reaching 17.034 billion square inches (17,034 MSI) in 2029. SEMI official announcement

What was announced

SEMI explains that AI-related demand is driving the growth in 2026 shipments. Specifically, this means "epitaxial wafers" used for leading-edge logic (chips for computing) and "polished wafers" for memory. SEMI says memory demand is spreading not only to high-bandwidth memory (HBM), which is drawing attention for AI, but also to server DRAM and enterprise NAND flash. SEMI official announcement

SEMI also says that recovering utilization at mature-node fabs and easing inventory adjustments on the customer side are supporting shipments this year. Looking ahead, it expects the expansion of AI infrastructure to support growth in silicon wafer demand through 2029. SEMI official announcement

The forecast numbers

Key terms made simple

A silicon wafer is a thin disk made of silicon that serves as the base material for semiconductors. Most chips are made by building circuits on top of it. The largest diameter is 300 millimeters. MSI stands for "million square inches." It is a unit that measures volume by area rather than by the number of wafers shipped, so wafers of different diameters can be compared on the same scale.

A polished wafer is a wafer whose surface has been polished to a mirror finish. An epitaxial wafer is a wafer with an additional thin crystal layer grown on that surface, and it is used for chips that require higher performance. These figures include polished and epitaxial wafers shipped by manufacturers to end users, and do not include non-polished wafers or reclaimed wafers. SEMI official announcement

How to read this forecast

SEMI created this forecast based on information from members of its Silicon Manufacturers Group (SMG). It is not an official forecast of the SMG or of any individual company. So it should be read strictly as an industry outlook. SEMI official announcement

Wafers are the very first material in chipmaking. More wafer shipments mean more processing downstream at fabs, such as deposition, lithography, and etching. It is a clue for reading upstream supply chain trends in the context of the whole industry.
