What happened? Commercial availability announced on September 14

On September 14, 2026, Cognichip announced the commercial launch of "ACI Enterprise," an AI platform that supports semiconductor design. The company's announcement names Renesas and SiTime as adopting companies. For Japanese readers, the closest connection is that Renesas has publicly disclosed using AI in its design work. However, September 14 is the date of this announcement. It does not show when the two companies started using it. Cognichip's official announcement

In customer comments in the announcement, a Renesas executive praises improvements in the time needed for specification analysis and for creating and verifying design assets. A SiTime executive describes using it for the digital part of mixed-signal ICs. These are statements published in Cognichip's announcement. They do not reveal the target products or whether it is deployed company-wide, and they cannot be read to mean that SiTime's entire analog circuitry is designed automatically. Why does it matter? AI support that spans from specification to verification

In semiconductor design, you first define "what the circuit needs to do" as a specification, then turn it into concrete circuit behavior. After that, you check whether it works as expected and adjust the design to meet conditions such as speed and power consumption. So the design work is not finished just because the code describing the circuit is written. The specification, circuit description, verification, and physical implementation all need to be connected. Synopsys's explanation of the design flow

According to Cognichip's product description, ACI supports importing specifications, generating circuit descriptions, building verification setups, analyzing root causes of problems, and optimization. Its uses also include modifying existing designs, new designs, and implementation on FPGAs. The key point this time is that support spanning several of these steps is being offered as a commercial service. However, this range of features is the provider's own description. It has not been proven that the same results can be achieved for every circuit. ACI product description

Key technical terms: RTL, logic synthesis, and PPA

RTL describes "how data moves inside a circuit"

RTL stands for Register Transfer Level. It describes registers, which temporarily hold data, and the processing and data movement between them. Think of it as writing an operation such as "add the input numbers and store the result at the next timing" in a language for circuits. It looks like code, but what it describes is an electronic circuit. It is easiest to think of it as the stage where the circuit's behavior is made concrete, before deciding where actual components go on the chip. Technical explanation of RTL

Logic synthesis converts a behavioral description into a circuit structure

In logic synthesis, RTL is converted into a form that represents logic gates and their connections. A logic gate is a basic circuit element that decides its output based on its inputs. The synthesis tool optimizes the structure according to conditions such as speed and area, then passes it on to the placement and routing steps. However, no errors in synthesis does not mean that every behavior in the specification has been confirmed. Separate verification is needed to check functional correctness. Explanation of logic synthesis and verification

PPA is three evaluation axes: power, performance, and area

PPA stands for Power, Performance, and Area. For example, even for circuits with the same function, a key design measure is whether power and footprint can be kept low while meeting the required speed. The PPA optimization that ACI promotes is support for improving these circuit conditions. Being able to generate code quickly and the generated circuit having good PPA are two things that must be checked separately. ACI optimization features, Explanation of optimization during synthesis

Background: From a 2025 concept to commercial availability

In a founder's blog post on May 15, 2025, Cognichip introduced its ACI concept, which puts physics-aware AI at the center of semiconductor development. The direction of reducing time, cost, and complexity was already shown then. The 2025 concept and the September 14, 2026 launch of ACI Enterprise are separate events. This news can be seen as the stage where a product following that concept became commercially available and its adopting companies were announced. 2025 concept explanation, This launch announcement

Technically, even with AI, the meaning of the steps does not change: describe the circuit's behavior, verify it, and move on to physical implementation. Also, an FPGA works by mapping a design onto already-manufactured, reconfigurable circuit resources. Because implementation is handled differently from an ASIC built for a specific purpose, getting something to run on an FPGA cannot be treated as the same achievement as finishing manufacturing of a new ASIC. From a design AI announcement to public documents and a learning setup

Once you understand design-support AI, it helps to keep separate notes on reading specifications, verifying generated results, and physical implementation, so you can follow the scope of announcements. Tools for rereading English public documents and learning generative AI for summarizing public information or writing documents are separate options. Compare home practice PCs based on software requirements too. Taking a general generative AI course or buying a PC does not give you access to Cognichip or semiconductor design skills.

Checklist before comparing: supported OS for HDL tools and simulators, required memory, licenses, and CPU requirements. Separate what you can try with your current setup from what needs upgrading before choosing candidates.

How far can the "design in a few days" report go?

Cognichip reports an evaluation by an anonymous customer in which one person went from a 55-page specification through design, verification, and PPA optimization in a few days, work that previously took a team about 4 to 5 months. This suggests a large reduction in work, but it is an individual case published by the company. Details such as circuit size, comparison conditions, completion criteria for verification, and computing resources used have not been confirmed, nor has any third party reproduced the result. It is not enough to generalize to time savings across the whole industry. Anonymous customer evaluation report

What was launched this time is a commercial platform for design support. It is not an announcement that a finished chip was manufactured or mass-produced in a few days. Even if circuit description generation and synthesis move forward, verification that checks conformance to the specification and the later implementation steps must be kept distinct. Describing it as physics-aware AI does not guarantee that design errors will not happen. When looking at results, what matters is which steps are included in the time saved.
