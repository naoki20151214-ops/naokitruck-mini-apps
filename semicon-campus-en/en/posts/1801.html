On September 30, 2026, Shinko Electric Industries announced that it had signed a contract to acquire the entire Mobara Fab in Mobara City, Chiba Prefecture, from Japan Display Inc. (JDI). After the acquisition, Shinko plans to transfer the west area to FICT and mainly use the east area itself. The goal is to build a new core site for advanced semiconductor packages used in products such as AI accelerators.Shinko Electric Industries announcement

What was decided on September 30, 2026?

What was finalized this time is a purchase contract for the fab and a contract to transfer part of the site to FICT after the acquisition. Shinko is acquiring the land, buildings, and related facilities, with a site area of 373,482.91 square meters. The west area to be transferred to FICT has a site area of 232,744.59 square meters.

The handover from JDI to Shinko and the handover of the west area to FICT are both scheduled for the end of March 2027. Shinko plans to start producing semiconductor packages at the Mobara Fab in fiscal 2028. However, this is a plan presented by the company. It does not mean production has already started.Contract scope and production start plan

JDI says it ended production at the Mobara Fab in November 2025. In February 2025, it had announced a policy to end panel production at Mobara and consolidate domestic production at its Ishikawa Fab. This deal is a case where another company takes over the assets of an LCD fab that has stopped production and uses them as a semiconductor-related site.JDI transfer disclosure 2025 fab restructuring policy

What is a semiconductor package?

A semiconductor package is a component that connects a finished semiconductor chip to a board or device. It involves the wiring that links the chip to external circuits, the paths that supply power, how signals travel, and how the chip handles heat and mechanical stress. For high-performance AI semiconductors, not only the chip but also the wiring density and electrical characteristics of the package matter.Shinko Electric Industries business overview

The flip-chip package at the center of this story is one way of connecting a semiconductor chip to a substrate. The terminals on the chip and the terminals on the package substrate are connected directly through tiny joints. Shinko describes this method as being for high-speed, high-density semiconductors such as CPUs, GPUs, ASICs, FPGAs, and high-end servers.Flip-chip substrate explanation

The technical term build-up method refers to a manufacturing method that stacks insulating layers and wiring layers one by one on a substrate. Stacked vias are the conductive parts that connect different wiring layers vertically. Using these, many wires can be placed in a limited area. However, fine processing and tight dimensional control are needed, which makes manufacturing more complex.Substrate manufacturing technology

Shinko has also published flip-chip bonding by thermocompression at a pitch as small as 40 micrometers, and bonding technology using low-temperature Sn-Bi solder. However, it has not been decided that these R&D examples will be brought directly into the Mobara Fab.The company's assembly technology

Why is this getting attention in the AI era?

Shinko says demand for flip-chip packages for advanced semiconductors such as AI accelerators is growing as AI spreads and the data center market expands. It also says larger products and more layers are increasing the manufacturing load. In addition to expanding capacity at its Chikuma, Takaoka, Kohoku, and Wakaho plants in Nagano Prefecture, it laid out a plan to make the Mobara Fab a new core site. This is the company's view of the market and a management decision. It is not independent proof that guarantees future orders or business results.Shinko Electric Industries plan explanation

Converting a former LCD panel fab into a semiconductor-related site is also meaningful. Existing assets such as land and buildings may be usable. On the other hand, equipment for LCDs cannot necessarily be used as-is for semiconductor package manufacturing. How much of the cleanroom, air conditioning, power, chemicals, and inspection equipment will be renovated still needs to be confirmed.

JDI has not disclosed the transfer price of the Mobara Fab. However, it plans to record an estimated gain on the transfer of about ¥51.4 billion on a consolidated basis and about ¥54.9 billion on a non-consolidated basis as extraordinary income in the fourth quarter of the fiscal year ending March 2027. These are estimates and may differ from the final financial figures.JDI expected gain on transfer

What is known now, and the limits

On October 1, 2026, Mobara City announced that it had been decided that Shinko and FICT would set up new business sites in the city. However, the city's announcement only shows the decision to locate there. It does not independently prove the start of production in fiscal 2028, specific production capacity, customers, or mass production results.Mobara City announcement

JDI also says it acquired the Mobara Logistics Center next to the Mobara Fab on September 30, 2026, and signed a contract to transfer it to Shinko together with the fab. The acquisition price of the logistics center was ¥40 million, and the handover was completed the same day.Disclosure on the logistics center

As of October 4, 2026, no primary sources could be found on the actual start of capital investment, line construction, prototyping, customer qualification, or mass production results after the contract. So "the fab purchase contract was signed" and "production for AI semiconductors will start" need to be considered separately.

Checkpoints going forward

  • Whether the property handover is completed as planned at the end of March 2027
  • Whether the capital investment amount, equipment setup, and renovation scope for semiconductor packages are announced
  • When prototyping, customer qualification, and the start of mass production can be confirmed as actual results
  • How large orders, production capacity, and utilization for AI accelerators turn out to be
  • How the estimated gain on transfer shown by JDI is finalized in its final financial results

The key point of this news is that a plan to convert a large, unused fab in Japan into an advanced semiconductor packaging site has taken concrete shape. On the other hand, whether the plan leads to actual production and sales cannot be judged without watching the progress of capital investment and mass production.
