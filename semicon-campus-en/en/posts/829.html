September 2: A memorandum of understanding on AI data center power

On September 2, 2026, Infineon Technologies announced that it had signed a memorandum of understanding with Skeleton Technologies on AI data center power. It covers power conversion, which changes the form of voltage and current, and energy storage technology that eases sudden increases in power demand. September 2 is the announcement date; the date the memorandum was actually signed was not given separately.Infineon announcement

There are two pillars of development. One is a next-generation solid-state transformer that uses SiC to convert medium-voltage AC to high-voltage DC. The other is a system that combines GaN and supercapacitors to limit peaks in power demand. What was announced is a plan for joint study and development, not the start of mass production of a joint product.Scope of joint development

What matters is power conversion and handling demand swings

An AI data center's power system has two jobs: converting electricity into the form needed, and responding to demand that changes over time. This partnership can be seen as a plan to combine the two. Infineon lists goals of higher efficiency, power density, and reliability, and lower total cost of ownership. Power density is how much power can be handled in a given size, and total cost of ownership is a measure of costs including operation after installation. These improvements are presented as company goals.Aim of the partnership

When AI servers start up or their load changes, power demand can rise temporarily. Panasonic Industry also describes the idea of releasing power stored near servers to ease these brief peaks. In other words, power design must consider not just average power use but also how much demand rises over short periods.Panasonic technical explanation

SiC and solid-state transformers handle power conversion

SiC is a semiconductor material called silicon carbide, and this plan uses Infineon's CoolSiC. The company's design materials describe SiC's ability to handle high voltages and fast switching as elements of SST design. Switching means controlling the flow of electricity by turning it on and off at high speed. Power conversion circuits use this switching to convert electricity into the form needed.Partnership announcement, SST design materials

SST stands for solid-state transformer. Unlike conventional transformers that run at grid frequency, an SST combines semiconductor power conversion circuits with an isolation transformer that runs at medium frequency. Despite the name "solid-state," it does not eliminate all magnetic components. The isolation transformer transfers power while electrically separating the input and output sides.Infineon SST design materials

The technical materials describe a stage that converts AC to DC, a DC-DC stage that provides isolation and voltage conversion, and an output stage suited to the application. AC is electricity whose current direction changes periodically, and DC is electricity whose direction stays the same. In this partnership, the companies intend to combine CoolSiC with Skeleton's power conversion and supercapacitor technology for converting medium-voltage AC to high-voltage DC.Technical background on conversion stages, Scope of joint development

Design separates the voltage coming into the facility from the voltage each semiconductor component handles. This is so parts can be chosen based on the required isolation, circuit configuration, and the power each module handles. You cannot judge the efficiency or size of a finished power system from the properties of SiC as a material alone.Conditions considered in SST design

GaN and supercapacitors smooth out power peaks

The other development target combines Infineon's CoolGaN with Skeleton's supercapacitors. GaN is a semiconductor material called gallium nitride. Here, the semiconductors that convert and control electricity and the device that stores and supplies electricity each play their own role. It does not mean GaN itself stores electricity.Partnership plan using GaN

A supercapacitor is a component that stores electricity and releases it when needed. In peak shaving, it charges when demand is low and supplies extra power when demand rises. For example, when server-side demand jumps suddenly, if a nearby storage device covers part of it, the sudden increase handled by the upstream power supply can be eased. Skeleton describes this kind of demand leveling through charging and discharging.Skeleton application description

Peak shaving and backup power during outages have different purposes and required operating conditions. Skeleton's application description also separates peak shaving with supercapacitors from backup with its SuperBattery. So you cannot conclude from this peak-shaving plan alone that it can also handle long power outages.Peak shaving and backup applications

No specific performance results have been published; specifications and test conditions are the focus

The available primary announcement does not give a launch date, mass production timing, customers, specific ratings, or conversion efficiency for a joint product. The absence of published performance results is different from no testing having been done. From the current announcement, the size of efficiency or cost improvements cannot be evaluated. The performance of Skeleton's existing products also cannot be treated as results of this joint development.Published partnership details

The effects of general technology also come with conditions. Panasonic explains that the effect of supercapacitor solutions depends on capacity, allowable voltage fluctuation, temperature, wiring, load conditions, and more. Its materials help explain the principle, but they are not evidence of third-party performance verification of the Infineon and Skeleton joint development.Design conditions for peak solutions

Points for future judgment are the target voltage and power range, the load conditions used to measure efficiency, and the size and duration of peaks that can be covered. If customer verification results or mass production timing are shown, it will be easier to see how far the development plan has moved toward real deployment. Adoption in Japan or supply plans for the Japanese market cannot be confirmed from this announcement. To understand this partnership, it is important to follow company improvement goals separately from measurement results with stated conditions.Partnership announcement, Conditions that affect the results
