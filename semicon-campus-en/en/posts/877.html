Construction began September 8, with Phase 1 planned for completion in 2029

On September 8, 2026, ASML, a supplier of semiconductor manufacturing equipment, announced the start of construction of a new industrial campus at BIC North in Eindhoven, the Netherlands. A groundbreaking ceremony was held the same day. The news is that a site to support future lithography tool supply has moved to the construction stage. The company expects Phase 1 to be completed in 2029.ASML's September 8 announcement

The development is planned in phases. The final size is about 350,000 square meters, and depending on future needs it could have workplace capacity for up to 20,000 people. Phase 1 will house at least 3,000 people and is mainly planned for teams moving from existing ASML sites. The figure of up to 20,000 people is not a commitment to new hiring. Also, the full final-scale development will not be completed all at once with this groundbreaking.Site scale and relocation plan

What is a lithography tool? A machine that copies chip circuits with light

Lithography (exposure) is the process of copying circuit patterns with light. According to ASML's technical explanation, a pattern carried by light is reduced by optics and projected onto a light-sensitive wafer. Picture shrinking a designed pattern and copying it over and over onto a surface that reacts to light. The transfer is repeated while moving the wafer, and it is used to form the structure of each semiconductor layer.ASML's explanation of lithography principles

A wafer is a thin plate that serves as the base for building semiconductor structures. Light-sensitive here means a material that reacts to light is used on the surface. Because circuits are built up in layers, besides the optics that copy fine images, precise mechanisms that move the wafer to the right spot and metrology that checks its position are also involved. It helps to think of a lithography tool as combining the function of shining light with the function of precise alignment.Optics and positioning technology behind lithography

The new site covers the manufacturing setup on the side that builds these tools. In terms of the supply chain, ASML supplies the tools, and the chipmakers using those tools make the chips. So this groundbreaking can be positioned as building the foundation for equipment supply that supports future chip production. The fact that construction has started does not mean customers' chip production will rise right away.Role of the new site, What lithography tools are used for

Why bringing production and logistics together matters

ASML plans to bring production, logistics, and support offices together at the new site. It says it will introduce an approach called a "flow factory" for TWINSCAN plants to make module production and installation more efficient. A module here can be understood as a functional unit that makes up the tool. The plan aims for a manufacturing setup in which parts and assembly units move through the process in a steady flow.Manufacturing approach and consolidation plan

What stands out in this announcement is the attempt to organize not just the size of the buildings but also how manufacturing is done. However, the efficiency gain is an expected effect described by the company. The announcement gives no figures for how many more tools will be produced per year or by what percentage efficiency will improve. There is also no basis for reading it as shorter tool installation times at customer fabs. Evaluating what it means for supply expansion requires results after the site starts operating.Announced plan and scope of effects

From a new lithography tool site to equipment and FSE jobs

When you read about construction starting on a new site, think of development, assembly, installation, maintenance, and explaining to customers as separate roles. A construction plan alone does not tell you whether there are job openings or what the hiring conditions are. Summarize your equipment or manufacturing experience by target, task, and results, and connect it to relearning mechanical, electrical, and control basics and to technical career counseling. If you aim to support overseas customers, general business English conversation is also an option to compare as preparation.

Phased development over time, and the difference between "2028" and "2029"

BIC North is a plan that has been in preparation for some time. ASML's explanation for local residents mentions a 2024 agreement with the City of Eindhoven and 2025 dialogue with residents. The new site is positioned as a long-term phased development that complements the existing Veldhoven site. With this history, this groundbreaking can be called a milestone in a plan built up through preparation.ASML's explanation for the local community

Care is needed when reading the schedule. Page 20 of the shareholder meeting materials dated April 22, 2026, planned the groundbreaking ceremony for Q3 2026 and the first employee move for Q1 2028. Meanwhile, the September 8 announcement gives the outlook that "Phase 1 will be completed in 2029." The first employee move and the completion of all of Phase 1 are different milestones. Comparing just these two dates, you cannot conclude that everything has been delayed by a year. We could not confirm whether the target for the initial move is still valid or how the schedule lines up.April 22 shareholder meeting materials, September 8 completion outlook

Next, watch the completion schedule and figures showing supply capacity

Local infrastructure is another point to check. The materials for residents describe issues under consideration such as site preparation and drainage, environmental permits, nitrogen regulations, and power grid congestion. However, this page still contains wording from before final decisions, and its publication and update dates cannot be confirmed. It can be used to understand the background of the plan, but it cannot prove how far permits or power transmission facilities have progressed.Explanation of local infrastructure

In future announcements, we want to see the schedule to completion as well as the actual start of operation, the increase in production capacity, and measured efficiency gains. The main announcement so far has not disclosed the total investment or its breakdown by tool type. You cannot conclude it is a plant dedicated to a specific lithography technology, nor treat more AI chip supply or an end to chip shortages as confirmed effects. The progress confirmed here is that construction has started on a site that will support future tool supply.Details of the groundbreaking announcement

Note that all the materials referenced in this article come from ASML. They serve to confirm the groundbreaking announcement, the company's future plans, and the technical principles, but they do not mean future productivity gains have been proven by a third party. The key to reading this news is to track the planned completion separately from actual production results.
