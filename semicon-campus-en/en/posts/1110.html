Announced on September 18: development and sample shipments

On September 18, 2026, Sumitomo Metal Mining announced that it had developed "SiCkrest," a 12-inch (300mm) bonded SiC substrate, and had shipped samples to semiconductor makers. This is progress beyond just developing a large substrate: the company has delivered samples for customers to evaluate. However, September 18 is the announcement date. The date development was completed and the actual shipment date have not been disclosed. Sumitomo Metal Mining's announcement

The "world's first" claim in the announcement is limited to a 12-inch SiC substrate made by the bonding method, based on the company's own research. It does not say this is a world first for SiC substrates in general. Also, what has been reached this time is development and sample shipments. The announcement does not say the 300mm product has been adopted for mass production or that a stable, large-volume supply has started. The announcement and the scope of "world's first"

What is SiC? A semiconductor material for controlling power

SiC, or "silicon carbide," is a semiconductor material. To understand this news, it helps to think of it as a material for "power semiconductors," which control the flow of electric power. Sumitomo Metal Mining explains that SiC is expected to reduce energy loss in uses that must handle large currents and high voltages, such as drive control in electric vehicles. The company's explanation of SiC applications

Another term, "substrate," is the base material on which semiconductor devices are built. The figure 300mm here is the diameter of this base. It does not show the size or performance of the finished semiconductor chips. The starting point for reading this announcement is to think about substrate development separately from the evaluation of the semiconductor products built on it. Official description of the 300mm substrate

The lower losses expected from SiC are also, first of all, background on the material and its uses. No test results were shown, such as how much power consumption dropped in an electric vehicle using this 300mm substrate. The potential of a material and its effect after being built into a product need to be confirmed with separate evidence. Background on applications, This announcement

"Bonding" is a technology that separates the roles of the surface and the base

The key feature of SiCkrest is its two-layer structure, which joins a thin layer of single-crystal SiC to a low-resistance polycrystalline SiC support substrate. "Single-crystal" means a structure where the crystal orientation is uniform, and "polycrystalline" means a structure made of many crystal grains. It is easier to grasp the setup if you think of a high-quality single-crystal layer on the surface side, supported underneath by a polycrystalline support substrate. SiCkrest product technology description

So "a substrate with a single-crystal layer" is not the same as "a substrate made entirely of single crystal." The achievement this time is developing a 300mm product using a method that bonds a thin single-crystal layer onto a separate support substrate. By looking not only at the substrate diameter but also at the structure used to achieve it, you can understand what is special about the technology. Official PDF explaining the two-layer structure

The company says this structure lowers resistance and suppresses degradation under current flow. Resistance is how hard it is for current to flow, and degradation under current flow means the characteristics worsen as current passes through. However, these are technical advantages the company presents, and these materials include no quantitative performance comparison for the 300mm product. We cannot turn them into numbers such as "resistance dropped by X%" or "lifetime increased X times." Advantages described by the company and what was disclosed

Why larger diameters matter, and the background so far

The company names better production efficiency and lower manufacturing costs as the goals of a larger diameter. The business meaning of this news is that a 300mm product aimed at those improvements can be read as having moved to the stage of customer evaluation. On the other hand, the fact that the diameter could be increased does not by itself let us conclude that actual manufacturing costs have fallen. Official materials showing the goals of a larger diameter

There were also earlier adoption announcements for SiCkrest. On December 22, 2025, Sumitomo Metal Mining announced its adoption in MOSFETs from Shindengen Electric Manufacturing. The product page also states that 6-inch and 8-inch sizes are available. However, this page shows no publication or update date, so it cannot be used as evidence of the supply status of the 300mm product. December 2025 adoption announcement, Product page listing available sizes

This background can be summarized as development and sample shipments of a 300mm product being added to the existing product lineup. However, past adoption cannot be read as adoption of the 300mm product. The recipients of these sample shipments have not been disclosed, and there is no basis for guessing that Shindengen received them. Past adoption, This announcement

Information needed to judge mass production is still missing

This announcement does not let us confirm the shipment volume of the 300mm product, customer evaluation results, the start of mass production, production capacity, or price. So even though sample shipments are progress, they do not prove that mass production is viable or profitable. For the performance advantages and the "world's first" claim, the evidence referenced here is the announcing company's materials, not results verified by an independent third party. Official announcement, Detailed materials

One thing to watch in future judgments is "yield." This is the share of products made that are good enough to use. Even if large substrates can be made, the economics depend on whether good products can be obtained consistently. Besides yield, these materials also do not show details on the quality of the bonded interface or long-term reliability tests, so more information is needed to assess whether cost reductions can be achieved. Next, watch the exhibit and progress in customer evaluation

The company plans to exhibit this substrate at booth G-2 at ICSCRM 2026, held at Pacifico Yokohama North from September 27 to October 2, 2026. As of September 21, this is a future plan, not information that the exhibit has taken place. One point to check going forward is whether more specific explanations of the 300mm product come out. English announcement with the exhibit plan

Beyond that, what matters is whether customer evaluation results, mass production timing, and figures on quality and cost are published. What we can confirm now is only the company's announcement of the development and sample shipment of a 300mm bonded SiC substrate. To judge how far it is from mass production, we need to follow information showing stable supply and economics, not just substrate size.
