On October 14, 2026, GlobalFoundries (GF) announced that it had been selected as the technology provider and operator of two new semiconductor factories (fabs) to be built at its site in Malta, New York. The project is a joint U.S.-Japan initiative, with up to $21 billion in funding expected. Japan will provide the investment, and GF will provide the technology and manufacturing operations. GF official announcement

What was announced

For the first fab, about $10 billion will go toward speeding up construction of a new plant that handles 300mm wafers. Operations are expected to start in 2029. GF says that at full capacity, it will add about 900,000 wafers per year to U.S. 300mm wafer production capacity. About $10 billion will also go to the second fab, which is planned to add similar capacity. GF official announcement

On jobs, GF expects about 27,000 jobs in construction and related industries, plus more than 1,000 advanced manufacturing jobs in New York's Capital Region. Construction is scheduled to start in 2026. The site is already permitted and can use existing infrastructure. New York State will also provide support through its "Green CHIPS" program. GF official announcement

Key terms made simple

A fab is a factory that makes semiconductors. A 300mm wafer is a silicon disk 300 millimeters in diameter. Many chips are made on it at the same time. The larger the wafer, the more chips you get from one wafer, so this is the mainstream size today.

GF says the new fabs will mainly serve demand from cars, industrial equipment, and infrastructure such as communications. In these fields, a long and stable supply matters more than the most advanced scaling.

Agreements announced at the same time

GF also agreed with the U.S. Department of Commerce to revise its existing CHIPS and Science Act award. $955 million will be redirected to investments in silicon photonics (chips that use light for communication), SiGe (silicon germanium), and advanced packaging in Malta, New York, and Burlington, Vermont. In a separate agreement, the Department of Commerce will receive about 4% of GF's shares. GF official announcement

Links with Japanese companies

The announcement also includes comments from Japanese companies. The CEO of Renesas Electronics said the company wants to build GF's New York capacity into its plans as an extension of its own production network, to secure a long-term, stable supply. The President and CEO of Tokyo Electron noted that the company has supported GF's fab operations for many years and said it is pleased to help expand advanced semiconductor production in the U.S. Apple, GM, Cirrus Logic, and others also provided comments. GF official announcement

What to watch next

GF says it will announce the construction start date and other details separately. Operations are expected in 2029. The actual production capacity and the manufacturing technologies to be used will become clear in future announcements. This announcement is at the planning stage and does not mean the fabs are complete or running.
