Learning mapLearn semiconductors step by step, from beginner to near-expert

This blog is a learning site for building semiconductor knowledge step by step, from beginner to near-expert level. Articles are organized by "chapter" and "level (number of stars)." If you're not sure where to start, read in order from Chapter 1.

Level guide

🌱

Beginner ★1

You know the terms and the overall flow, and can explain them to others

🔧

Intermediate ★★–★★★

You understand how things work and what the numbers mean, and can read the news on your own

🎓

Advanced ★★★★+

You can read primary sources and explain the reasons behind a judgment
