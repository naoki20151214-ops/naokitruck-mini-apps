This is a site where Naoki, who has a humanities background, relearns semiconductors from the basics, the technology that is essential in the AI era, and organizes what he learns in words that beginners can understand.

Semiconductors are used everywhere in modern society: smartphones, PCs, GPUs, generative AI, data centers, cars, and factory equipment. But the field has a lot of technical jargon, and for people with a humanities background or no semiconductor experience, the first step can be very hard.

On this site, Naoki and Professor Owl talk things through in a dialogue format. We explain as carefully as we can "why it works that way in the first place," "how a process connects to the steps before and after it," and "what kinds of jobs that knowledge leads to."

The main topics are semiconductor basics, semiconductor manufacturing processes, AI chips and GPUs, company research, job types and career change, and term explanations. Our goal is to connect technical understanding with career understanding.
