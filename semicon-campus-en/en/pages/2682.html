Chapter 5: Transistors and ScalingSmaller means faster. But electricity leaks. How has the industry solved this?

Smartphones got faster and their batteries last longer because transistors kept getting smaller. But as they shrink, a new problem grows: electricity leaks even when the switch is off. In this chapter, you will learn, step by step, what the problem is and how it has been solved (by changing materials and by making the shape 3D).

When you finish, you will be able to explain in your own words what the number in "2nm" means and why GAA became necessary after FinFET.
