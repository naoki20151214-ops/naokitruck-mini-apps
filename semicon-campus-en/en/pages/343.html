About this site

Semicon Campus is a site that explains semiconductors, which are essential in the AI era, from the basics in words that beginners can understand.

Semiconductors are used everywhere in modern society, from smartphones, PCs, GPUs, generative AI, and data centers to cars and factory equipment. However, the field has a lot of technical jargon, and taking the first step can be hard for people from non-technical backgrounds or with no semiconductor experience.

On this site, we sometimes use a dialogue format and explain as carefully as we can "why it works that way in the first place," "how a process connects to the steps before and after it," and "what kinds of jobs that knowledge leads to."

Our main topics are semiconductor basics, semiconductor manufacturing processes, AI semiconductors and GPUs, company research, jobs and career changes, and glossary explanations. We aim to connect technical understanding with career understanding.

Purpose of this media site

We organize information from semiconductor basics to manufacturing processes, equipment, and the latest news so that readers can understand it step by step. In our job articles, we provide material to help you think about which job types connect with experience in manufacturing, maintenance, quality, and similar fields. Pushing readers to apply for jobs or change jobs quickly is not our goal.

Research and sourcing policy

For technology and news, we prioritize primary and official sources such as company announcements, official materials from equipment and materials makers, public agencies, research institutes, and industry associations. We separate announced facts from this site's explanations and outlooks, and we check dates, figures, product names, and process names. We list references within the article or at the end.

For jobs and job postings, we refer to company career pages, public job postings, government occupational information, and similar sources. Job duties and working conditions vary by posting, so always check the latest official information before applying.

Use of AI and human review

We may use AI for organizing research, drafting outlines, writing drafts, and checking wording. Our policy is not to treat AI output as a source in itself and to check important claims against reference materials.

The editorial team is responsible for the publishing policy and corrections. Because some News articles are created and scheduled for publication automatically, we do not claim that a human reviewed every article one by one before publication. Even after publication, humans review sources, facts, links, and reader feedback, and make any necessary corrections.

Corrections

If you find a typo, a mismatch with a source, or outdated information, please send the article URL and the relevant part through the contact form. The editorial team will recheck the primary sources and revise the text as needed. For corrections that affect conclusions, we show in the article what changed and the date it was checked.

Advertising and partnerships

To keep the site running, we may display ads and affiliate links. Ads and PR are labeled so readers can recognize them. When we introduce services, we put checking job duties, conditions, and cases where a service may not be a good fit first.

Using this site

This site provides general information for learning and understanding jobs. Product specs, job postings, compensation, and application requirements may change. For important decisions, please check the latest official materials from each company or public agency.
